Walls and openings read from the model

Monthly-average-day solar (NASA POWER radiation, isotropic sky). Direct sun is ray-traced against the building's own elements, neighbour blocks and tree crowns (25% light through a crown); diffuse sky light is not reduced. Heat into the room = U × absorptance × solar × 0.04 m²K/W (steady state; thermal mass delays and damps it).
